About me.

I’m Brad. I live in Riverton, and I sing and play acoustic guitar around the Salt Lake Valley.

I’ve played guitar for about seven years. The songs I play are ones a room already knows: hits from the 80s, 90s and 2000s, classic rock, and older singalongs like Jolene and Ring of Fire. Each one is arranged for one guitar and one voice.

I’ve played in bands too. I ran one called Grimstone, playing hits from the 80s, 90s and early 2000s, and I ran our sound, so I bring my own and handle it myself. I’ve also played with School of Rock’s adult program in Sandy.

I write my own songs too. A couple of them are on my YouTube channel.

Away from music I work in health research. That side is on the work page.
