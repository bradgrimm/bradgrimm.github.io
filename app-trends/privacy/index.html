APP TRENDS

Privacy.

The short version

App Trends is a private dashboard I use to follow the numbers for my own apps, websites and YouTube channels. It isn’t offered to anyone else.

  • It signs in only to accounts I own, with read-only access. It can’t post, edit or delete anything.
  • From YouTube it reads channel-level totals: views, watch time and subscriber counts. It doesn’t read comments, messages or anything about individual viewers.
  • What it reads is kept on my own computer and in my private dashboard. It isn’t sold, shared or used for ads.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google account’s security settings, under third-party connections.
