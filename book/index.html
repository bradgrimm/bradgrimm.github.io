Good to know

  • SoundI bring my own and run it myself: two Yamaha speakers for patios and bigger rooms, or a small acoustic amp for quiet ones.
  • PowerNone needed. Everything runs on a battery that lasts the whole event. If there’s an outlet, I can plug in.
  • SpaceA flat spot about 6 by 6 feet. Shade or cover if it’s outside.
  • Set length30 minutes to two hours. One set or two, with a short break in anything over an hour. Need longer? Ask.
  • Setup15 minutes to set up and 10 to pack up. I’m ready before your guests arrive.
  • WeddingsCeremony and cocktail hour, up to 90 minutes. I’ll learn your processional and recessional with three weeks’ notice.
  • WhereBased in Riverton. I play the Salt Lake Valley and Utah County. Farther out, ask.
  • RatesThey depend on length and date. Ask and I’ll send a number within a day or two.
