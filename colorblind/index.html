What’s inside.

See

Protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia and achromatopsia in real time, from published research models. Drag the split line to compare.

Match?

Pin one color, point at another, and get a plain answer: same, close or different, with a heads-up on pairs colorblind eyes often mix up.

Test

Trace the number on each plate, or take the cone contrast test that checks red, green and blue separately.

Learn

Short lessons on how color vision works, each type of color blindness, and designing for it.

Help.

Is the test a diagnosis?

No, it’s a screening. If your results suggest a color vision deficiency, see an eye care professional.

Do I need the camera?

No. Tap the photo button in See to use a built-in sample photo or one of your own.

Does the app collect data?

No. See the privacy policy.
