COLORBLIND VISION

Privacy.

The short version

Colorblind Vision doesn’t collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.
  • The app doesn’t connect to the internet.
  • The camera image is processed on your device in real time. It isn’t recorded or sent anywhere. A photo is saved or shared only when you choose to save or share it.
  • Photos you open from your library are read on your device only.
  • Test results and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Questions

Use the form on the support page.
