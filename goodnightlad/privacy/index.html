App privacy.

The short version

The apps don’t ask for names, emails, or accounts, show no ads, and don’t sell or share personal information.

Camera

The camera is used only to recognize the pages of the book and place the animations on them. Camera images are processed on the device and are not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Third-party components

The apps are built with Unity and PTC Vuforia Engine. These components may send limited technical information (such as device model, operating system version, and app version) for licensing, crash reporting, and performance. It is not used to identify you or for advertising.

Children

The apps are made for families with young children. Links that leave the app are behind a grown-up check.

Questions? Use the help form.
