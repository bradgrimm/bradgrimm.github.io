MAKING MUSIC TOGETHER

A small studio.
A place to play.

My small home studio in Riverton has been a place for band practice, working on songs, and figuring things out together. It’s where I record ideas, experiment with guitar tones, and make room for other musicians.

With Grimstone, I played drums, hosted band practice, and helped pull our shows together. I loved having people over to turn a list of songs into a set we could actually play.

These days, I’m focusing on guitar and vocals, building a solo acoustic set, and writing songs of my own. I’m also looking for musicians to play with, whether that’s a rehearsal, a collaboration, or a spot in a band.
