Holiday parties · 2026

Booking holiday parties through December 23.

Office parties, family Christmas parties, neighborhood open houses. I play the same songs, with Christmas ones mixed in: Run Rudolph Run, Last Christmas, Feliz Navidad, Jingle Bell Rock.

An hour is about right for an office lunch. A full party is two sets with a short break, and I’ll keep it quiet enough that people can still talk.

Ask about your date

A long list of singalongs, and it keeps growing.

I pick songs by one test: do people start singing before they notice they’re doing it. Sweet Caroline passes. So does Jolene, and the opening riff of Sweet Child O’ Mine on an acoustic.

Every song is arranged for one guitar and one voice. Requests are welcome.

What you get

Songs your crowd knows
Hits from the 80s, 90s and 2000s next to older singalongs. Background music or the main event, your call.
My own sound
I bring it and run it. It’s battery powered, so I don’t need an outlet.
30 minutes to two hours
One set or two, with a short break in anything over an hour. Need longer? Ask.
A small footprint
A flat spot about 6 by 6 feet. 15 minutes to set up, 10 to pack up.
Your volume
Background to mid. I’ll check the level with you once things get going.
Close to home
Based in Riverton. I play the Salt Lake Valley and Utah County. Farther out, ask.
