Salt Lake Valley and Utah County

Salt Lake open mics.

Got a song and a free night? Here’s where to play it.

Every night on this list was checked against the venue’s own calendar or a current listing, and each one shows the day it was last checked. Rooms that go quiet drop off after two months, so what’s here is what’s running. Plans can shift, so a quick tap on the source before you load the car is always smart.

  • Every Monday, 7:30 PM

    Scion Cider Bar

    Salt Lake City (Central 9th) · 916 S Jefferson St, Salt Lake City

    Free. 21+ with ID. Performers and the crowd are all in a weekly drawing for concert tickets.

    Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source ↗

  • Every Tuesday, sign-up 6:30 PM

    Sugar House Coffee

    Sugar House · 2011 S 1100 E, Salt Lake City

    Free, all ages. Four-minute slots, first come, first served. Music, poetry and comedy all welcome.

    Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source ↗

  • Every Wednesday, 8 PM

    Velour Live Music Gallery

    Provo · 135 N University Ave, Provo

    Two songs each. Get there by 7 PM to lock in a spot. $3 to play, $4 to watch. A legendary room to cut your teeth in.

    Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source ↗

  • Second Wednesday of the month, 7 PM

    Tyler Library (Salt Lake County Library)

    Midvale · 8041 S Wood St, Midvale

    Free, all ages, drop in. Music, poetry, comedy and prose. A brand-new series.

    Next one: Wed, Jan 13, 2027

    Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source ↗

  • Second Thursday of the month, 6:30 to 9 PM

    Land of a Thousand Hills Coffee, Daybreak

    South Jordan · 10678 S Lake Run Rd, South Jordan

    15-minute slots, booked ahead with the request form on the Daybreak page. Family-friendly sets.

    Next one: Thu, Dec 10, 2026

    Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source ↗

  • First Friday of the month, 7 PM

    Provo City Library

    Provo · 550 N University Ave, Provo

    Acoustic only. Sign-ups start at 6:30 PM. Free.

    Next one: Fri, Dec 4, 2026

    Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source ↗

  • One Friday a month, 6:30 to 8:30 PM

    Magna Library with the Magna Arts Council

    Magna · 2675 S 8950 W, Magna

    Free, all ages, show up and sign up. First-timers and old pros side by side.

    Next one: Fri, Jan 22, 2027

    Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source ↗
