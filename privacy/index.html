Privacy.

The short version

This site doesn’t use analytics, ad trackers or cookies of its own, and I don’t sell or share anything you send me.

  • Booking requests. The booking form sends your name, email, event date, location and message through Formspree, which keeps a copy and emails it to me. I use it only to reply about your event.
  • Email. If you write to me directly, I keep the message only to answer it.
  • Videos. Embedded videos use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode, so YouTube doesn’t set cookies until you press play. Once you play one, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Hosting and fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Like any web server, they see your IP address and browser details when a page loads. I don’t see or store that.
  • Links out. Links to GigSalad, social media and the app stores take you to sites with their own privacy policies.
  • Apps. Each of my apps has its own privacy page, which covers what that app does. They’re listed under Books & Apps.

Questions or removal

To ask what I have from you, or to have it deleted, email booking@bradgrimm.com.
