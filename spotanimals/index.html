What’s inside.

14 worlds

A jungle temple, a sunken pirate ship, a snowy Antarctic camp, a busy zoo, an autumn orchard, and more.

560 finds

Each find plays the next note of a little tune. When the last one turns up, everything comes out to dance.

Stickers

Earn a sticker every five finds and stick it anywhere on the world pictures.

Easy or Normal

Normal asks for the five things in the tray. In Easy, anything hidden counts.

Help.

How do I play?

Pick a world. The tray at the bottom shows five things hidden in the picture. Tap one when you spot it. Pinch to zoom and drag to look around.

Too hard?

Switch to Easy on the home screen. There’s no tray, and any hidden thing you tap counts.

Where are my stickers?

Tap the sticker button on the home screen, pick a world, and drag your stickers onto its picture. Drag one off the picture to put it back.

Music off?

Use the music button on the home screen or in the pause menu.
