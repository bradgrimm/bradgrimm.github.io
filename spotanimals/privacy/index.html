SPOT! ANIMALS

Privacy.

The short version

Spot! Animals doesn’t collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.
  • The app doesn’t connect to the internet.
  • Game progress (stickers earned and where you placed them, and settings like Easy/Normal and music on/off) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • The app doesn’t use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Questions

Use the form on the support page.
